*AI Security · Published October 11, 2026*

Imagine a helpful assistant reading a pile of notes. One note contains instructions that try to make it abandon its task.

**AI security** protects AI systems and the applications around them against misuse, unauthorized access, and harmful actions.

## What is prompt injection?
It is an attempt to make an AI behave in unintended ways through instructions in a request or material it reads. That material might be a document or webpage.

## Why do permissions matter?
An AI that can only draft text has different risks from one that can send messages, change accounts, or delete files.

Limit the data and tools an AI can access. Use application-level permission checks, validate outputs before passing them to other systems, and require approval for sensitive actions. Keep secrets out of prompts.

A sentence telling an AI to “never reveal private data” is not enough to enforce access security. Controls outside the model must protect that data.

## Try it on paper
Design a fictional study assistant. Should it read public lessons, private payment records, or all learner messages? Give it only what its task requires.

**Remember: Helpful AI still needs boundaries, testing, and monitoring.**

## 60–90 second video
Show a study assistant encountering an untrusted note. Illustrate limited tools, an application permission check, and human approval for sensitive actions.
Suggested pacing: 10 seconds for the everyday analogy, 25 seconds for the concept, 25 seconds for the access example, and 10 seconds for the takeaway.
Use large captions and slow speech. Explain acronyms before using them. Keep analogy scenes separate from computer-system scenes.

## Paper lab
Draw a fictional assistant with a public lesson library and three possible tools. Mark which data and actions are allowed. Add a human approval boundary for sensitive actions. This is a design exercise, not an exploit test.
Record the request, relevant evidence, decision, and reason. No accounts, configuration changes, or paid software are needed.
