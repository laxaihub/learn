*AI Foundations · Published October 11, 2026*

AI can help us learn and create. A few simple habits help us use it more carefully.

## 1. Pause before sharing
Keep passwords, home addresses, private photos, and personal documents out of your requests. Protect other people’s information, too.

For example, ask: “Help me write a birthday invitation.” You can add the real names and address yourself afterward.

## 2. Check before trusting
A confident answer can still be wrong. Compare important claims with a trusted book, official website, or knowledgeable person.

Realistic pictures, voices, and videos can also be made or changed with AI. If a message asks urgently for money or a password, pause and contact the person through a number you already know.

## 3. Ask a person
Children should explore AI with a trusted adult using suitable tools. If an answer is upsetting or confusing, stop and talk to someone you trust.

For health, money, or safety decisions, get help from a qualified person.

## Try it without an account
Compare these requests:

- “Here is my password. Help me remember it.”
- “Suggest a way to organize my weekly chores.”

Which one protects your privacy better? Why?

**Remember: Pause. Check. Ask.**
