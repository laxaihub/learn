*AI Foundations · Published October 11, 2026*

Imagine teaching a computer to tell apples from bananas.

You show it many pictures, each with a label: “apple” or “banana.” During training, it adjusts how it makes predictions to better match those labels.

Later, you test it with pictures it has not seen before. Can it still tell the fruits apart?

This is one way AI learns, called **supervised learning**. There are other ways, too.

## What could go wrong?
Suppose every apple in the training pictures is red. A green apple might confuse the system.

That is why varied examples and careful testing matter. Getting training examples right does not mean it will get every new example right.

## Try it without a computer
Draw apples and bananas on six cards. Show a partner four labeled cards, then ask them to sort the other two. Add a green apple. What clues helped?

This activity illustrates learning from examples. A person and an AI do not learn in exactly the same way.

**Remember: Better examples and careful testing help us build better AI.**
