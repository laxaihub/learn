*AI Foundations · Published October 11, 2026*

Imagine asking a computer: “Write a four-line story about a hamster exploring the moon.”

It gives you a story to read and change.

That is **generative AI**: a type of AI that creates content, such as words, pictures, music, or videos, using patterns learned during training.

## What is a prompt?
A **prompt** is the request or instruction you give an AI tool.

“Write a story” is a prompt. “Write a funny four-line story about a hamster on the moon, using easy words” gives more direction.

Clear instructions can help, but they do not guarantee a good or correct result.

## A story is different from a fact
Inventing a moon adventure is useful when you want fiction. Inventing information is a problem when you need facts.

AI can produce made-up information that sounds believable. Check factual claims before sharing them.

## Try a tiny activity
With a trusted adult if needed, ask a permitted AI tool for the hamster story. Then ask it to make the story funnier. Which version do you prefer? Add your own ending.

**Remember: AI can make a draft. You decide what to keep and change.**
