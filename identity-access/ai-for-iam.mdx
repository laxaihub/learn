*Identity & Access · Published October 11, 2026*

Imagine a school librarian noticing that one card is suddenly being used in an unusual way. They investigate before deciding what happened.

AI can help identity teams notice patterns across many sign-ins and access records. For example, Microsoft Entra ID Protection uses machine learning in its risk calculations.

## What can AI help with?
It can flag suspicious activity or suggest permissions that deserve review. These signals help people and security policies focus attention where it may be needed.

An unusual sign-in is a warning, rather than proof that someone is an attacker. Travel, a new device, or other legitimate changes can affect the situation.

## Who makes the decision?
An organization defines policies. Depending on those policies, a risk signal may trigger more verification, a block, or investigation. People should check errors and the effect on legitimate users.

If an AI assistant helps with access administration, its own permissions should be limited. Important changes need appropriate approval and an audit record.

## Try it on paper
Invent three sign-ins: a usual device, a newly replaced device, and a suspicious pattern. What evidence would you investigate before acting?

**Remember: AI can highlight risk. Evidence, policy, and oversight make access decisions accountable.**
