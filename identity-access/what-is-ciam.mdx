*Identity & Access · Published October 11, 2026*

Imagine joining an online learning site. You create an account, sign in, and open your lessons. If you forget how to sign in, you need a safe way back.

**CIAM means Customer Identity and Access Management.** It focuses on identity experiences for customers using websites and apps, including registration, sign-in, and account recovery.

## Why does it matter?
Customer sign-in should be understandable and accessible. It also needs protection against account takeover. Collect only the information needed, and explain how it will be used.

## Signing in is only part of the story
A customer account proves an identity; it does not automatically prove that a person bought every course.

In a possible LaxAIHub design, CIAM handles learner sign-in. The application separately checks course enrollment before delivering paid lessons. Hiding a button alone would not enforce that rule; the server must check permission.

## Where does Microsoft fit?
Microsoft Entra External ID offers customer identity capabilities. A customer sign-in experience and staff administration need different permissions and policies.

## Try it on paper
Sketch four screens: create account, sign in, recover access, and open a course. Where should identity checks happen? Where should course permission be checked?

**Remember: A smooth sign-in and careful access checks work together.**
